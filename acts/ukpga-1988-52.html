ukpga/1988/52

Road Traffic Act 1988

1988 CHAPTER 52 • UK Public General Acts

1. Dangerous driving

A person who drives a mechanically propelled vehicle dangerously on a road or other public place is guilty of an offence.

2. Dangerous driving penalty

A person guilty of an offence under section 1 above is liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine or both.

3. Careless, and inconsiderate, driving

If a person drives a mechanically propelled vehicle on a road or other public place without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration for other persons using the road, he is guilty of an offence.
